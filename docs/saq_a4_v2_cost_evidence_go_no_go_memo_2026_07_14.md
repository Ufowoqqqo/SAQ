# A4 V2 Cost/Evidence Reopening Memo

Date: 2026-07-14

Decision: **GO_PROTOCOL_DESIGN**

Maximum current outcome: **PROTOCOL_READY_NOT_AUTHORIZED_FOR_EXECUTION**

## Decision in one paragraph

Primary work supports a new protocol that measures complete four-arm
diagnostic-instrument construction separately from independent verification
and archival research evidence, provided that all remain visible and
verification is complete.
It does not support reclassifying the old A4-1S no-go, timing only the inner
solver, hiding instrument-required intermediate-bundle materialization, or
accepting hash-only evidence.
The old full-pipeline result remains terminal. The proposed V2 is a new,
falsifiable construction-feasibility study and is not authorized to execute.

## Why reopening is scientifically defensible

The predecessor asked whether one particular verbose exact
construction/evidence pipeline fit a budget. More than half of its measured
CPU was research-only canonical shard serialization, and the remaining
`scalar` bucket still mixed construction with independent replay and parsing.
Thus it did not isolate either solver cost or construction of the compact
intermediate bundle required by the registered diagnostic.

Official SAQ source review also rules out a stronger interpretation. The A4
panel bundle is not byte-compatible or estimator-equivalent to SAQ's persisted
factors, short/long codes, ids, centroids, rotations, and metadata. The
positive target is the **A4-reference-equivalent four-arm diagnostic bundle**
whose model fields, labels, B4/B8 payload, decoder, and lookup semantics were
frozen at pre-outcome commit `3aa2f6e`, before cost result `9ce1052`. It is not
a current-SAQ representation or a full-vector ANN index. V2 therefore does
not call it retained/deployable SAQ or claim production index-build
affordability.

The primary-source review supports this predeclared decomposition:

```text
B_build         = frozen build and binary materialization
P_parity        = pre-run exactness and representation fixtures
T_instrument    = setup + full four-arm computation + intermediate bundle
V_replay        = independent semantic verification
E_evidence      = E_emit + E_archive_body
T_study_metered = B_build + P_parity + T_instrument
                  + V_replay + E_evidence
F_trailer       = disclosed finite reporting self-reference exclusion
```

The primary gate may use `T_instrument` because its claim is only whether this
frozen comparative instrument is admissible under the internal resource cap.
Every other metered term remains mandatory reported evidence. If parity,
replay, or evidence finalization does not complete, there is no scientific
decision.

## Anti-rescue constraints

V2 must retain, before any execution:

- the `8192 x 128` PCG64 panel;
- all 128 coordinates and `K=1..256` curves;
- exact rational objective and tie semantics;
- both rates, 64 groups, all allocations, all eight block starts, full
  encoding and packing;
- all four candidate/control arms and their intermediate bundle bytes;
- the inherited one-panel cap of `34,560,000,000` CPU microseconds, with no
  claim that `5/2` projects GIST/CIFAR cost under the new phase boundary;
- the same machine/toolchain identities once implementation is frozen; and
- the predecessor native-child/IPC/required-parse construction architecture;
- one logical observation with no best-of-retries and cumulative interruption
  CPU; and
- full independent all-record replay.

It may remove only research-schema redundancy: full DP predecessor rows,
duplicate producer/replay values, repeated manifest fragments, and repeated
arrays plus repeated subarray/file hashes. Every coordinate/cardinality keeps
one complete optimum record, and every block start keeps one complete
schema-frozen trajectory record. Intermediate codebooks, metadata, labels,
payloads, and bundle writes cannot be reclassified as research evidence. The
semantic fields and decoder contract remain those of pre-outcome `3aa2f6e`;
only their canonical V2 container and research-evidence schema are newly
frozen.

## Reviewer-facing claim ceiling

A V2 synthetic pass would establish only:

> On the frozen machine, toolchain, exact-rational scalar/allocation semantics,
> and deterministic bit-level block/encoding semantics, the complete four-arm
> synthetic diagnostic instrument fit its internal one-panel admission cap and
> an independent implementation reproduced every registered decision.

It would not establish natural-data prevalence, a block-VQ advantage, ANN
quality, candidate-only affordability, recall, QPS, end-to-end SAQ index-build
cost, SAQ integration, or novelty. It would permit only asking
whether to write a new base-only protocol. It would not revive or execute the
old A4-1P gate.

## Current authorization

The accompanying protocol may now be written, reviewed, committed, and
reported. Implementation, synthetic execution, benchmark-data access, and
SAQ modification remain forbidden until a separate user instruction names
the authorized V2 stage.
