Scope: all data_aware* result directories in this worktree, including earlier recovery attempts.
Sum each completed GNU time record once by filesystem inode; retries and repeated tests count separately.
Do not add diagnostic resources.tsv or per-arm compute times to GNU-time wrappers: these overlap.
Empty/unfinished resource logs are active-or-interrupted, not completed zero-cost work. Their consumed CPU is unknown until GNU time closes.
240 CPU seconds is the configured reserve for unmetered commands; it is an allowance, not a measured cost or proof all unknown work fits.
Remaining allowance excludes currently active unreported CPU and must be recomputed before another job starts.
GNU time records are rounded to 0.01 seconds. Failed or timed-out commands still consume CPU and are included.
Largest reported RSS is the largest single-job GNU-time maximum, not aggregate simultaneous task RSS.
Summing wall durations would double-count overlaps; elapsed recovery deadline is enforced separately.
Read-only snapshot is not a completion assertion or an experimental decision.
